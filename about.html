Send one dataset and one question you want ranked.

One file — CSV or Parquet, up to 100,000 rows. One question, phrased as a ranking: which of these should we look at first, and why. Not two questions. Not a whole warehouse.

Free at that size. I reply within one business day, and you get the work back within three business days. Message me on a Saturday and you'll hear from me Monday. Anything larger than the cap gets a quote first, not silence.

What you might get back instead of a score

What comes back is what your data can and cannot support, before any quote. If the label can be reconstructed from the features, or the feature window overlaps the outcome window, I'll tell you that instead of sending a score. That is a more common outcome than people expect, and it is cheaper to hear from me than from a model in production.

What to put in the email

  • The file, or a link to it.
  • The one ranking question, in a sentence.
  • What the rows are — pages, accounts, tickets, SKUs.
  • Which column is the outcome, if you already know.
  • Roughly when each column is recorded, if you know. This is what decides whether there's a leak.

If you don't know the last two, send it anyway and say so. Working that out is part of the job.
